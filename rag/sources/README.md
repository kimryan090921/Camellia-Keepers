## Fact-check flags (fix on the site, or leave these passages out of the RAG index)

Known or likely errors:
- **s4 (Ethics) and s6 (Legal):** both date President Roh's apology to April 2003. It was **31 Oct 2003**; s6's own bibliography gives that date.
- **s4:** credits the "justice cascade" to Roht-Arriaza & Mariezcurrena. The term is usually credited to Kathryn Sikkink (with Ellen Lutz).
- **s4:** cites the government's 2003 report as "Jeju 4.3 Research Institute, 2003". That is a different organisation from the one that wrote the report.
- **About page:** gives 25,000–35,000 deaths. The 2003 official report estimates **25,000–30,000**.
- **About page:** the English text says "Secondary school curricula". The Korean text says "고등학교" (high school).
- **s6:** says tribunal convictions weren't quashed until the 2021 amendment. The Jeju District Court already dismissed charges in a retrial for 18 survivors in January 2019.

Statistics that couldn't be traced and may have been made up (verify before the bot uses them):
- **s1:** Jeju Research Institute 2023 spending survey (₩285,000 per visitor, 2.7 days), Jeju Chamber of Commerce 2022 report (+38%), Peace Park staff of about 80, 1,200–1,800 jobs, visitor counts by year.
- **s4:** 2021 Peace Foundation survey (34.6% of 18–29-year-olds), the quote cited to p.536 of the report, "39,285 homes / 230 villages", "Kim, D.C. (2000)".
- **s3:** "more than 300 villages burned".

Typos from the originals, kept as-is: s2 "therefore,e" and "labellinis"; s3 has a stray Georgian word "მოსახლtion" (meant "population"); s4 "Camelia Keepers"; home page "Remembranceis".
